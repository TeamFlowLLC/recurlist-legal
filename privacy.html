RecurList — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

RecurList (“the App”) is provided by TeamFlow LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the RecurList apps on iOS and Android.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and password (your password is stored securely in hashed form by our authentication provider and is never visible to us), an optional display name, and a unique account identifier.
  • Your shopping data. The stores, items, lists, quantities, and notes you create in the App, and the purchase history you record — including item names, prices, and dates.
  • Receipt scanning (camera). When you scan a receipt, the photo is read entirely on your device to extract the text. We do not upload or store the receipt image. Only the text you choose to save (such as item names and prices) is stored as part of your shopping data.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health information, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics/tracking services.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the App and keep your lists and history in sync across your devices and the members of your household.
  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App’s core features.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How your information is stored and shared

  • Hosting. Your data is stored in our backend hosted by Supabase, Inc., which provides database and authentication services on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit.
  • App updates. App updates are delivered through Expo (Expo, Inc.). Delivering the correct update may involve basic technical information such as app version and device type.
  • We share information with these service providers only as needed to run the App. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.
  • We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

4. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and shopping data as described in Section 5.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access and update. You can view and edit your stores, items, and purchase history in the App at any time.
  • Delete your account and data. In the App, open Catalog → Delete my account to permanently delete your account and all associated data. You may also request deletion by emailing MonkeyscabStudios@gmail.com; we will complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EEA/UK), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at MonkeyscabStudios@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

6. Children’s privacy

RecurList is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and secure authentication, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
